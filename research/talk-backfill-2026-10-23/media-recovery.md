# Slides and video recovery checklist

Prepared **24 October 2026**, after the confirmed backfill.

This lists **32 distinct links discovered during the talk research**, plus **2 additional archival leads**. An appendix lists **38 further media URLs already referenced by the older archive**. Repeated URLs appear once, with all relevant delivery IDs.

Use the first section for retrieval work. Tick recovered items and add the filename or replacement URL beneath them. The [CSV version](media-recovery.csv) has `status` and `retrieved_path` columns if you prefer a spreadsheet. These files do not automatically synchronise edits.

**Download availability still needs checking** for most external targets. Historical addresses are preserved exactly; Wayback links open capture searches. The example Battersea talk page was checked and still exposes the recorded SlideShare and Vimeo links.

The site has direct links to these older slide/video pages even where it cannot embed the media. A SlideShare page or course index is not necessarily the original PPT/PDF. Preserve which delivery a recovered file belongs to: a repeated talk can have different slide revisions. The linked source/programme pages may also provide your original abstracts for descriptions.

## Retrieval hints

- **SlideShare:** your account and the numeric embed ID may help locate original uploads or downloads. Keep the old URL with any recovered file.
- **Old personal and Mongers sites:** try the original URL, then the provided Wayback lookup. An archived HTML slide index may have separate images or downloads.
- **Video:** retain the event/date and any session timestamp. T076 is part of a longer lightning-talk recording.
- **Existing Google Slides/PDFs:** the appendix includes current archive references. Full Idiomatic Perl and Tieing/Overloading PDFs are already linked there; older extracts may still differ.
