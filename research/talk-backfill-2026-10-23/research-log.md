This is a documented research pass, not a claim to have found every appearance.

names. Sources identifying Dave only as an attendee, organiser, quoted mailing
list participant, or author of a book were not treated as proof he spoke.
Unrelated people with the same name were excluded.

## Follow-up: German workshop, cruise, and FOSDEM

Dave supplied these memories on 23 October 2026: he went straight from the
Italian Code Jam onto a Geek Cruises Mediterranean voyage; he spoke at a
German Perl Workshop, possibly in 2001, with titles possibly “Idiomatic Perl”
and “Idiotic Perl”; and he spoke at FOSDEM roughly twenty years ago. These
memories directed the follow-up searches; programme evidence below supplies
the dates without treating tentative recollections as exact metadata.

### German Perl Workshop

The current organiser's [2001 archive summary](https://www.perlworkshop.de/archiv/2001/)
and [2002 archive summary](https://www.perlworkshop.de/archiv/2002/)
give event dates and the Fachhochschule Bonn-Rhein-Sieg venue, but no talks.
Older Act sites link to former `/de/2001/` and `/de/2002/` addresses, which

The [official timetable captured on 5 April 2002](https://web.archive.org/web/20020405194515/http://www.perlworkshop.de/2002/times.epl)

Perl** on **15 February 2002, 09:00–10:30 and 11:00–12:30**, room hs1 for the
tutorial. The [official abstract list captured on 5 January 2002](https://web.archive.org/web/20020105225714/http://www.perlworkshop.de/2002/contriblist.epl)
attributes contributions 100 and 82 respectively to CROSS, Dave. Both are
added as T105–T106. The tutorial is represented as one delivery with 180
teaching minutes and an explicit grouping note.

Dave's [25 January 2002 message](https://www.mail-archive.com/london.pm%40london.pm.org/msg04789.html)
describes the previous night's London.pm Idiotic Perl talk as a practice run
for the German workshop. His [17 February message](https://www.mail-archive.com/london.pm%40london.pm.org/msg05602.html)
independently places him at the workshop that week. This supports **2002**,
rather than the tentative 2001 recollection. The [archived 2001 abstract list](https://web.archive.org/web/20011226234848/http://www.perlworkshop.de/2001/contriblist.epl)
was also retrieved; no Dave Cross / CROSS, Dave attribution was found.
Michael Schwern's 1999 “Ineffective Perl Programming, or Idiotic Perl” is a
different speaker's presentation and was excluded.

## Implementation prerequisite — issue #26

After the review, Dave requested implementation of issue #26. The local
database now has a nullable `presentation.title`, with a supported incremental
migration and regenerated ORM mapping. Presentation display titles fall back
to the talk title when blank; event pages, recent-presentation cards and the

and URLs are unchanged.

The full regression suite and site build passed. Comparing the migrated
archive with the pre-migration backup confirmed every original column and row

checks passed. Generated files were identical to a pre-change baseline build.

accepted T093 correction have not yet been written to the database. See the
